Stay & sail

Two things worth knowing about beyond the restaurant. Both are run by our neighbours on the beach — we're happy to put you in touch.

Our focus is the table — but guests often ask where to sleep and how to get out on the water. Here's what we recommend.

Accommodation

Rooms a short walk away

A small number of simple, quiet rooms near the beach — thatched roofs, shaded verandas, sea breeze. Managed independently of the restaurant; we'll connect you directly.

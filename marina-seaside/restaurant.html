The Restaurant

A room with the bay in it

Makuti overhead, teak underfoot and an open side that frames the beach, the dhows and the yachts.

The setting

Built from the beach up

The pavilion is timber and thatch, open on every side so the breeze runs straight through. Rattan chairs, woven pendants and driftwood details keep it soft and unpolished — the kind of room you sit in for four hours without meaning to.

Tables run from the shade of the makuti roof out to the terrace railing, where the beach and the anchorage open up below. At lunch it is bright and blue; by 17:30 the whole room turns amber and the lanterns come on.
